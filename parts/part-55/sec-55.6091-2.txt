Title 26 CFR — Part 55—EXCISE TAX ON REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUSTS AND REGULATED INVESTMENT COMPANIES
Subpart C—Procedure and Administration
## § 55.6091-2 Exceptional cases.

§ 55.6091-2 Exceptional cases.

Notwithstanding the provisions of § 55.6091-1, the Commissioner may permit the filing of any Chapter 44 tax return in any local Internal Revenue Service office.
