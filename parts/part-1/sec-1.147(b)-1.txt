Tax Exemption Requirements for State and Local Bonds
## § 1.147(b)-1 Bond maturity limitation-treatment of working capital.

§ 1.147(b)-1 Bond maturity limitation-treatment of working capital.

Section 147(b) does not apply to proceeds of a private activity bond issue used to finance working capital expenditures.
